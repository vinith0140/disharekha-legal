Delete your data

Destin · Updated November 20, 2026

Destin has no accounts. Most of your information is stored only on your phone, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

On your phone

  1. Open Destin and go to Home.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my data, then confirm with Delete.

This deletes your choices, saved paths, compare list, AI coach chats and settings, and gives the app a new random app ID. Uninstalling the app deletes them too. It does not delete what our server holds about your old app ID; for that, see below.

Information we hold

To delete the information we hold about your use of the app (which salary pages and PDF reports you unlocked, your Plus record and its history, and the phones linked to it), email vinith0140@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Destin data. Please include:

  • your App id, shown at the bottom of the Destin Plus page in the app (open it before you tap Delete my data, because that gives the app a new ID), and
  • if you bought Plus, the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with "GPA.").

We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records at Google Play. Google keeps its own record of what was bought, under its own policies. Our own copy is deleted when you ask.
  • Your Plus subscription. Deleting your data does not cancel Plus. To cancel, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can bring Plus back on the same Google account with Restore purchase.
  • Reported answers are not linked to you, so they cannot be found and deleted one by one.

Good to know

  • Deleting your data does not refund purchases. For refunds, contact Google Play.
  • Your AI coach questions are not kept on our server, so there is nothing to delete there.
  • Even if you don't ask, our server deletes these records by itself after a time; see "How long we keep it" in the Privacy Policy.
