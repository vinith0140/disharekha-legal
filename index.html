Privacy Policy

Disharekha · Effective November 19, 2026

Disharekha is an independent app that helps students in India choose what to study and do after Class 10 and Class 12: courses, branches, jobs, salaries, entrance exams and admissions. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to what the app needs.

Information we collect

  • No account. You use the app without signing up. We never ask for your name, phone number, email or photo. Your choices (state, class, subjects, language), saved paths, compare list, AI coach chats and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • A random app ID. The app creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, phone number or Google account. It is used to count your free salary pages, free PDF reports and AI coach questions, to link Plus to your phone, and to prevent misuse.
  • Pages you unlock. To count your free salary pages and free PDF reports, we keep which salary pages and reports were opened with your app ID.
  • Purchases. If you buy Plus, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. We receive and keep the purchase details Google Play gives us (which plan, the Google Play order number, the price, a purchase token, until when it is paid, its status, and when these changed) to switch Plus on and off.
  • Your questions to the AI coach (Plus only). When you ask the coach a question, your question, your recent chat in that conversation, the page you asked from, and the details you chose in the app (such as your state, class, subjects and quick answers like your goal, or a district if you type one) are sent to our server and to an AI service to write the answer. We do not keep your chats on our server; they are kept on your phone. To answer some questions (for example, which colleges you can join), the AI service may search official websites.
  • Answers and pages you report. If you tap Report under a coach answer: the question, the answer, the reason you chose and the app language. If you tap Something wrong on this page?: which page it is, the reason you chose and the app language. Reports are not linked to your app ID.
  • Error notes. When something fails in the app (for example the coach, a purchase, a PDF report or a crash), the app sends a short note: where it failed, a short error code, the app version and the language. It is not linked to your app ID, and we only keep a count of each note per day.
  • Technical information. Like any app, basic technical data (such as your IP address) is processed by our hosting provider to deliver the app, its data updates and the coach. We do not use advertising IDs.

Listen to this page uses your phone's own text-to-speech. Nothing is sent to us for it.

How we use it

  • To run the app and keep its course, job and admission information up to date.
  • To count your free salary pages, PDF reports and coach questions.
  • To switch on Plus after a purchase, and switch it off when it ends.
  • To answer your questions in the AI coach.
  • To fix answers and pages you report, and problems the error notes show.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent misuse.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't share your information for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers only to run the app: Google Play (purchases), Cloudflare (hosting our server) and OpenAI (the AI coach). They process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms. Under OpenAI's terms for its API, the questions we send are not used to train its models, and OpenAI may keep them for a limited time for safety checks. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and storage

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and processed with established cloud providers, which may be in India, the United States or other countries. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Coach questions are not kept on our server after the answer is sent.
  • Daily counts of coach questions are kept for one day.
  • The pages you unlocked and your Plus purchase record are kept as needed to count free pages, for billing, refunds and misuse prevention, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Reported answers and pages are kept until they have been checked; we keep at most the latest 2,000.
  • Error note counts are kept for 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete everything the app saved on your phone any time: Home → Delete my data (at the bottom of the page).
  • The AI coach is optional. You can use every course, job and admission page without it.
  • Ask us to delete the information we hold: see how to delete your data.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or ask any privacy question, by email (below).

Students under 18

Disharekha is made for students in Class 10, Class 12 and college, and for their parents and teachers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. We do not ask for a name, phone number or email from anyone. Plus is a paid subscription: a student under 18 should buy it only with a parent's or guardian's permission, and the app asks for this before the purchase.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
