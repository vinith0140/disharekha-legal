Privacy Policy

Destin · Effective November 24, 2026

Destin is an independent app that helps students in India choose what to study and do after Class 10 and Class 12: courses, branches, jobs, salaries, entrance exams and admissions. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to what the app needs. Destin is independent: it is not a government app and is not connected to any government body, exam body or college. It shows information from official sources; always check the official notice before you apply.

Information we collect

  • No account. You use the app without signing up. We never ask for your name, phone number, email or photo. Your choices (state, class, subjects, language), saved paths, compare list, AI coach chats and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • A random app ID. The app creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, phone number or Google account. It is used to count your free salary pages, free PDF reports and AI coach questions, to link Plus to your phone, and to prevent misuse. Our server stores only one-way scrambled codes made from it, not the ID itself.
  • Pages you unlock. To count your free salary pages and free PDF reports, we keep which salary pages and reports were opened with your app ID.
  • Purchases. If you buy Plus, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. We receive and keep the purchase details Google Play gives us (which plan, the Google Play order number, the price, a purchase token, until when it is paid, its status, and when these changed) to switch Plus on and off, and which phones use the purchase (up to 3, by the scrambled code of their app ID).
  • Your questions to the AI coach (Plus only). When you ask the coach a question, your question, your recent chat in that conversation, the page you asked from, and the details you chose in the app (such as your state, class, subjects and quick answers like your goal, or a district if you type one) are sent to our server and to an AI service to write the answer. With each question the AI service also gets a one-way code made from your app ID (not your name), so it can spot misuse. We do not keep your chats on our server; they are kept on your phone. Please don't put personal details such as your name or phone number in your questions. To answer some questions (for example, which colleges you can join), the AI service may search official websites.
  • Answers and pages you report. If you tap Report under a coach answer: the question, the answer, the reason you chose and the app language. If you tap Something wrong on this page?: which page it is, the reason you chose and the app language. Reports are not linked to your app ID.
  • Error notes. When something fails in the app (for example the coach, a purchase, a PDF report or a crash), the app sends a short note: where it failed, a short error code, the app version and the language. It is not linked to your app ID, and we only keep a count of each note per day.
  • Technical information. Like any app, basic technical data (such as your IP address) is processed by our hosting provider to deliver the app, its data updates and the coach. To apply daily limits against misuse we count requests per network using a one-way scrambled code of the network address, not the address itself, and delete these counts the next day. Our hosting provider keeps short request logs for a few days to run and secure the service. We do not use advertising IDs.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos. Listen to this page and reading a coach answer aloud use your phone's own text-to-speech; nothing is sent to us for them.

How we use it

  • To run the app and keep its course, job and admission information up to date.
  • To count your free salary pages, PDF reports and coach questions.
  • To switch on Plus after a purchase, and switch it off when it ends.
  • To answer your questions in the AI coach.
  • To fix answers and pages you report, and problems the error notes show.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent misuse.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't share your information for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers only to run the app: Google Play (purchases), Cloudflare (hosting our server) and OpenAI (the AI coach). They process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms. Under OpenAI's terms for its API, the questions we send are not used to train its models, and OpenAI may keep them for a limited time for safety checks. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and storage

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and processed with established cloud providers, which may be in India, the United States or other countries. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Coach questions: not kept on our server after the answer is sent. OpenAI may keep them for a limited time for safety checks.
  • Daily counts (coach questions, and requests per network): until the next day.
  • Which salary pages and PDF reports you unlocked: deleted once your app ID has not been used for 12 months.
  • Your Plus purchase record, its history and the phones linked to it: while Plus is active, and deleted 400 days after the paid time ends (for refunds, support and disputes).
  • Reported answers and pages: up to 180 days, and at most the latest 2,000.
  • Error note counts: 30 days.
  • Server request logs at our hosting provider: a few days.
  • Anything above can be deleted sooner if you ask (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything the app saved on your phone any time: Home → Delete my data (at the bottom of the page).
  • The AI coach is optional. You can use every course, job and admission page without it.
  • Ask us to delete the information we hold: see how to delete your data.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask any privacy question, by email (below). We reply within 30 days.
  • You can stop at any time: stop using the coach, delete your data, or uninstall the app.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you may also name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

Students under 18

Destin is made for students in Class 10, Class 12 and college, and for their parents and teachers. Under Indian law (the Digital Personal Data Protection Act), anyone under 18 is a child. If you are under 18, please use Destin with your parent's or guardian's knowledge. Plus is a paid subscription: a student under 18 should buy it only with a parent's or guardian's permission, and the app asks for this before the purchase. The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

We do not ask for a name, phone number or email from anyone. Destin shows no ads, does not track students across other apps or websites, and does not build profiles of anyone's behaviour for advertising. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's data at any time (see "Contact and complaints" below).

If something goes wrong

If a data breach affects your information, we will tell you in the app and inform the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
